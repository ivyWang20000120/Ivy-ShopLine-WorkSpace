# Avalara e-invoicing Plugin Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 14, 2026  
**Effective date:** October 14, 2026  
**Operator:** Chengdu Silk Software Co., Ltd. (成都思而科软件有限公司)

Avalara e-invoicing Plugin (the **App**) helps SHOPLINE merchants manage electronic invoicing through an Avalara connection and, where configured, electronic waybills through the selected solution. The App supports checkout invoice-information collection, market-specific invoice rules, forward and reverse documents, document retrieval, invoice-change requests and approvals, access roles, operation logs, and email-template options. The merchant configures the applicable markets, seller details, triggers, and operating mode.

This policy explains how we collect, use, retain, disclose, and protect information processed through the App.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to installation, authorization, configuration, and use of the App in SHOPLINE; the App's communications with SHOPLINE and Avalara; checkout data used for invoicing; electronic invoice and waybill processing; document access and email-template links configured by merchants; and related merchant support.

## 2. Our Role in Processing

For store, order, buyer, and invoice information processed on a merchant's behalf, the merchant generally determines why and how the information is used. We process that information as a service provider under the merchant's authorization and instructions. We may separately process merchant account details, App access records, support communications, security information, and service-operation records to operate and protect the App.

End customers who have questions or wish to exercise privacy rights concerning an order or invoice should generally contact the merchant first. We will assist the merchant as required by applicable law and our service arrangements.

## 3. Information We Process

The information processed depends on the permissions granted, the merchant's configuration, the market, and the features used.

### 3.1 Store and account information

- SHOPLINE store name, store domain, store or merchant identifiers, and App installation or connection status.
- Avalara connection settings, including the configured client credentials and environment (Sandbox or Production). Credentials are used to connect the App to Avalara and should not be shared in support requests.
- App user accounts, assigned roles, account status, and login-related activity needed to manage access.

### 3.2 Checkout, order, and fulfillment information

- Buyer-selected invoice type and invoice details entered at checkout, which may include name, address, country, personal tax ID, company name, company tax ID, tax office, and other fields enabled by the merchant for a market.
- SHOPLINE order identifiers and numbers, order status, order and payment details, currency and amounts, product names, SKUs, quantities, prices, and fulfillment information.
- Shipping address and country, tracking number, tracking company, and other shipment data needed for invoice or e-waybill processing.
- Product serial number or IMEI data when the merchant enables the option to append that data to invoice product names and the relevant data is available from SHOPLINE.
- Refund or cancellation information used to evaluate or trigger a reverse-invoice process under the configured market rules.

### 3.3 Seller, market, and integration configuration

- Seller legal name, tax number (including VKN where applicable), tax office, address, document-number series, Avalara sender aliases, and other seller compliance details entered by the merchant.
- Market-specific checkout field settings, invoice triggers, reverse triggers, e-waybill settings, invoice labels and legal disclaimers, and supported solution selections.
- Payment-gateway mappings and carrier mappings, which may include carrier legal name, tax number, and an optional vehicle license plate.
- Seller logo and signature images uploaded for document presentation.
- SHOPLINE email-template options and configuration for showing invoice entry points on order pages.

### 3.4 Documents, change requests, and operation records

- Invoice and e-waybill records, including document numbers, document type, status, attempt history, timestamps, Avalara identifiers, delivery status, and links to related orders or fulfillments.
- Generated or retrieved document files, such as invoice and waybill PDFs, and the information contained in those files.
- Invoice modification requests, including the current and requested invoice profile, reason for the request, submitter, reviewer, approval or rejection decision, rejection reason, execution note, and audit timeline.
- Operation-log entries for actions such as login, webhook receipt, document submission or completion, profile synchronization, approvals, and related metadata.

### 3.5 Information you provide directly

We may process information in support requests, emails, feedback, or troubleshooting materials that a merchant chooses to send us. Please do not send passwords, full payment-card details, or customer information unrelated to the support issue.

### 3.6 Information we generally do not collect

The App is not designed to collect full payment-card numbers or CVV codes. Payment authorization and processing are handled by SHOPLINE or the merchant's payment provider. The App processes payment method or gateway information only when needed for invoice mapping and document presentation.

## 4. How We Use Information

### 4.1 Provide invoicing and waybill functions

- Receive SHOPLINE order, fulfillment, and refund events and apply the active market rules to determine whether a forward invoice, reverse invoice, or e-waybill workflow should run.
- Use merchant-configured Avalara credentials to submit invoice information and receive processing status or document references; when a separate e-waybill solution is configured, share the relevant shipment and seller information with that solution.
- Collect checkout invoice details according to the merchant's market field settings and seller profile.
- Support automatic invoicing and merchant-managed manual invoicing workflows, including historical-record review and queued submission where available.
- Organize documents by order and fulfillment so merchants can search records, review timelines, refresh status, and download available PDFs.
- Apply the Turkey e-Arşiv fallback only when the merchant enables it and the configured eligibility conditions described in the App are met.

### 4.2 Manage invoice changes and access

- Create and review invoice modification requests, compare current and requested values, record reasons and decisions, and maintain an audit trail.
- Authenticate App users and apply role-based permissions to user, document, rule, system-setting, log, and approval functions.
- Record key App actions to support operational troubleshooting and accountability.

### 4.3 Maintain the service and provide support

- Connect the App with SHOPLINE and Avalara, maintain configuration and document status, and troubleshoot service or integration errors.
- Respond to merchant questions, protect the App from misuse, and maintain service reliability.
- Meet legal requirements and respond to valid legal process where applicable.

## 5. Legal Bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing may rely on performance of the merchant service agreement, the merchant's instructions and authorization, compliance with legal obligations applicable to the merchant or service, legitimate interests in providing and securing the App, or consent where required. Merchants are responsible for determining their legal basis for collecting buyer invoice details and configuring the App for each market.

## 6. Electronic Invoice and Waybill Records

Electronic invoices and waybills may contain buyer, seller, order, tax, payment-method, product, and shipment information. Issued documents and their identifiers may be subject to accounting, tax, or electronic-document retention rules that vary by country. We retain and handle these records as needed to provide the App and meet applicable legal and contractual requirements. A merchant's uninstall request does not override a retention obligation that applies to an issued document.

Invoice changes are handled through the App's request and approval workflow. The original document, related change request, decision, and audit information may be retained as necessary to preserve the document history and satisfy applicable requirements.

## 7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The App may use cookies, local storage, session identifiers, or similar technologies needed for authentication, session continuity, security, and service operation when used in the SHOPLINE admin or related App pages. Browser controls can be used to manage cookies, although disabling required technologies may prevent some App functions from working.

## 8. How We Share Information

We do not sell merchant or buyer information.

- **SHOPLINE:** The App exchanges store, checkout, order, fulfillment, refund, and configuration information with SHOPLINE as needed to provide the enabled functions.
- **Avalara:** When the merchant connects Avalara and invokes an applicable workflow, the App sends the information required to submit an invoice or waybill operation and receives processing results and document references.
- **Waybill solution providers:** If a merchant enables an independent third-party carrier or waybill solution, relevant shipment and seller information may be shared with that solution to issue or process the waybill.
- **Service providers:** Information may be processed by infrastructure or support providers engaged to host, maintain, secure, or support the App. The provider list and processing locations depend on the deployed service configuration.
- **SHOPLINE email templates:** If the merchant enables the invoice PDF link and installs the generated template code, the resulting SHOPLINE email may include an invoice-download link. If the merchant disables the App's invoice email option, the generated template switches to an informational message without that link.
- **Legal, safety, or business transfers:** We may disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or security, or as part of a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or asset transfer, subject to applicable law.

## 9. Data Retention

We retain information for the period needed to provide the App, maintain document and audit history, respond to support requests, protect the service, and meet applicable legal or contractual obligations. Retention periods for issued invoices and waybills depend on the rules applicable to the merchant and the relevant market. For information about the current retention periods applicable to a store or record category, merchants may contact us using the details below.

## 10. International Transfers

SHOPLINE, Avalara, and service providers may process information in countries or regions different from the merchant's or buyer's location. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers under applicable law.

## 11. Security

We use administrative and technical measures appropriate to the information and service, including authenticated access and role-based permissions within the App. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Merchants should protect their SHOPLINE and Avalara credentials, assign only necessary App permissions, and promptly disable access for users who no longer need it.

## 12. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law, merchants and buyers may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of personal information, or to withdraw consent. Merchants can manage App users, roles, market settings, and optional features in the App. Buyers should generally send requests about order or invoice information to the merchant that processed the order. We will assist merchants with requests as required by law and our service arrangements.

## 13. Uninstallation and Deletion Requests

Uninstalling the App stops the merchant's use of its functions but may not immediately remove issued documents or records that must be retained for legal, accounting, security, or audit purposes. We handle deletion requests under applicable law, platform rules, and retention requirements. To submit a request, email [apps@silksoftware.com](mailto:apps@silksoftware.com) with the subject **Data Deletion Request** and identify the store and data concerned. Do not include passwords or payment-card details.

## 14. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with SHOPLINE and Avalara. A merchant may also enable a third-party e-waybill or carrier solution. These services operate under their own privacy notices and terms. Merchants should review those notices and confirm which integrations are enabled for their stores.

## 15. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for SHOPLINE merchants and business workflows and is not directed to children. If you believe a child's information has been provided to us through the App, contact us so we can review the request.

## 16. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the App, our processing, or legal requirements. We will update the date above and provide notice of material changes through reasonable channels where required.
